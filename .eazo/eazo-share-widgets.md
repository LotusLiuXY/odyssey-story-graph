# Eazo Share Widgets

## App
Premise: The Odyssey · Story Graph — an interactive, pan/zoom parchment map of Homer's Odyssey. The homeward journey of Odysseus is laid out as five plot beats along a hand-inked dashed sea route; tapping a beat unfolds its attached characters, places, and themes, and any node opens a bottom drawer with background, relations, and an original-text highlight.
Visual identity: aged parchment world-map. Warm parchment ground (#d7d1b3) and lighter warm paper (#efe0bc); deep sepia ink (#643631) for text and the plot backbone; wax-seal red-brown (#9c3b2e) for characters, sea-green (#4f7c6b) for places, ochre-gold (#b0842f) for themes. Display type Cinzel (classical serif), body Spectral / Noto Serif SC. Hand-inked dashed routes, circular seal-style node badges, subtle paper-fibre grain.

## Scenario: story_graph
Available facts: headline (reading the whole Odyssey as one map), result (ten-year homecoming in five plot beats), details (each beat unfolds its characters, places and themes; covers gods, heroes, monsters, islands and themes)
Composition: lead with the app title and the compass-marked plot backbone (numbered 1–5 seals along a dashed route); support with a small cluster of colour-coded child seals (character / place / theme) fanning off one beat, and the tagline "一张图读懂整部《奥德赛》 / Reading the whole Odyssey in one map".
Visual cues: palette parchment #d7d1b3 + ink #643631 with seal accents #9c3b2e / #4f7c6b / #b0842f; type classical serif (Cinzel) headings on warm paper; motif — dashed hand-inked S-curve route threading circular wax-seal nodes, faint aged-paper grain and vignette.
